The cost of running a feeder empty

Every reel change is a chance to lose a component

When a component reel runs out mid-run, the feeder waits, the board stops, and the operator rushes to re-thread. Splicing tape turns that changeover into a non-event.

Changeover downtime

Every empty reel means a stopped placement line — minutes lost every single shift, multiplied by every feeder.

Component dropouts

If the tape tail is not held, the last components miss pickup and your board ships short.

Manual splicing inconsistency

Hand-applied joins vary in alignment and pressure; a weak splice breaks mid-run.

ESD damage

Non-conductive or non-dissipative tape can charge and discharge into sensitive SMD parts.

Sprocket-hole misalignment

A poorly aligned splice jams in the feeder, stopping the machine instead of saving it.

SKU fragmentation

Different feeders need different widths — buying from multiple sources multiplies qualification work.

How to splice a reel

The four-second splice that keeps the feeder running

Splicing joins two carrier-tape ends so components feed continuously. The splice tape must align the sprocket holes, hold firmly and stay thin enough to pass the feeder.

01Prepare the tail of the running reel and the head of the new reel
02Align the sprocket holes and overlap the two tape ends
03Apply the splice tape over the joint and press firmly
04The feeder advances continuously — no stop, no dropped part

Technical evaluation FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering

What is SMT splice tape used for?

Splice tape (also called leader tape or splicing tape) joins the tail of one component carrier tape to the head of the next reel. This lets the pick-and-place feeder advance continuously through a reel change without stopping the line or dropping components.

Which tape widths do you supply?

Standard widths are 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and 44 mm, with 5, 7, 9.1, 18 and 25.3 mm options. The width must match your feeder and carrier-tape pitch. Send your feeder model and tape width to confirm.

Does it work on both paper and embossed plastic tape?

Yes. The PE base with high-tack rubber or polyester-resin adhesive bonds to both paper and embossed plastic carrier tape. The 0.05 mm thin profile passes through feeder rollers without jamming.

Is anti-static / ESD-safe splice tape available?

Yes. Anti-static variants are available in yellow, black and black+yellow. Use the ESD-safe option when splicing reels that feed static-sensitive components.

How is it packed and how fast can you ship?

Common widths pack 500 pieces per box; wider formats pack 250 pieces per box. Standard colors ship from stock; custom logo printing and non-standard widths are confirmed per order.

Can I print my logo or a part number on the tape?

Yes, custom logo or identification printing is available. Specify the print content and tape width when requesting a quotation.
