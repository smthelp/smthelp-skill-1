Technical evaluation FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering

What panel sizes can the S300AS route?

The cutting range is 600 mm × 400 mm with 400 mm Z clearance and 100 mm Z travel. Confirm your panel dimensions and component height — if your board is larger, we can recommend the next model in the series.

How is the cutting path programmed?

Two ways: teach programming from the handheld LCD controller, or import a DXF/PLT file with automatic path optimization. Trajectories support points, straight lines, arcs, full circles, curves, polylines, helices and ellipses.

What is the accuracy and edge quality?

Repeatability and cutting accuracy are ±0.02 mm. The 800 W precision milling spindle produces neat, stable edges without burrs — the key difference versus manual cutting.

How fast is it compared with manual depaneling?

Manual depaneling is typically about 60 boards per hour. The S300AS dual-table alternating cycle reaches about 120 boards per hour, and the operator only needs to load panels and press start.

Does it handle V-cut, stamp holes and odd-form boards?

Yes. The milling cutter routes V-cut panels, stamp-hole arrays and odd-form contours. Different fixtures (bakelite, purpose-made) hold the board during the cut; send your panel for a free test.

What about warranty and support?

The S300AS includes a 1-year free warranty and free prototype testing before purchase. Power input is full voltage AC 110–220 V, with an RS232 external control interface for integration.
