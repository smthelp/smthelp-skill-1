The hidden cost of scrap tape

Manual tape cutting is quietly taxing your SMT line

Every finished reel leaves a long tail of carrier tape. Left unmanaged, it steals operator time, clutters aisles and undermines the 5S and ESD discipline your customers audit.

Operators as waste handlers

Manually scissoring tape at every reel change pulls skilled operators away from value-added placement work, shift after shift.

Tape drifting everywhere

Uncut scrap tape tangles around feeder carts and aisles, creating a housekeeping problem that shows up in every customer audit.

Scissors mean cut risk

Hand-cutting thousands of tape ends per day exposes operators to blade injuries that automated cutting eliminates.

Mixed tape formats

Paper tape and embossed plastic tape behave differently; one fixed manual routine rarely handles both cleanly.

Bins filling too fast

Loose, uncut tape fills waste bins in hours, forcing repeated line-side trips just to empty them.

Consumable drain

Cheap cutters dull quickly and jam, trading a small purchase price for endless blade swaps and downtime.

Technical evaluation FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering

What types of tape can the S-680A cut?

Paper carrier tape from 8 to 32 mm wide and embossed plastic tape. Very thin vinyl tape is not recommended. If you run an unusual tape format, send a sample description with your inquiry and we confirm compatibility before quotation.

Which pick-and-place brands does it fit under?

Installation references include Hanwha / Samsung SM411, 421, 471, 481, 482, plus, S1 and S2 feeder carts, JUKI KE-series trolleys, Sony lines and domestic mounter brands. The body length can be customized to match your cart footprint.

How does automatic mode work?

Rotate the emergency stop switch to the on position, then press ON at AUTO. The blade unit starts moving and cuts tape continuously until the collection bin is removed or the emergency stop is pressed.

What routine maintenance is required?

Empty the collection bin as required, replace the blade when cutting quality drops, check the helical gear every 3 months, lubricate the LM shaft as required, and check belt tension monthly. The high-speed steel rotary blade design delivers up to 4× the lifecycle of conventional cutters.

Is it safe for operators?

Yes. The cutting path is enclosed, an emergency stop is on the front panel, and the machine removes the need for manual scissor cutting — the cut-risk task it replaces was the hazard.
